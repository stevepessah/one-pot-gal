Ryan and Kathleen made this cioppino (an Italian-American fish stew) during the summer of 2019, at our annual Dewey beach vacation. We sat in the kitchen at Anchor Way enjoying a crisp glass of summer rosé while this recipe came together - with only the best seafood that the Delaware shore has to offer!

We recommend serving in a shallow bowl so that the seafood is the star - and don’t forget a hunk of bread to mop up the broth. You can also serve this over pasta.

![Dewey Beach Cioppino](images/dewey-beach-cioppino-1.png)

## Ingredients

- 1 large fennel bulb, thinly sliced
- 1 large shallot, thinly sliced
- 1/2 yellow onion, sliced
- 5 fat cloves of garlic, minced
- Red pepper flakes
- Extra virgin olive oil
- 2 cups white wine
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt and fresh cracked pepper
- 2 small cans good quality whole tomatoes (or stew your own - break up with spoon while cooking)
- 1.5-2 quarts seafood stock (we bought one and then made more stock with water and the shells from the shrimp)
- 4 tablespoons of tomato paste
- Juice and zest of 1 lemon
- 1 dozen fresh clams (live not frozen)
- Container of jumbo lump crab
- 1.5lb of fresh jumbo sea scallops
- 1 lb of large shrimp
- Fresh basil
- Fresh parsley
- 1/3 stick of butter
- 1 bay leaf
- Sourdough bread for dipping

## Instructions

- Sautée the fennel, onion, shallot, garlic, red pepper flakes, and bay leaf in EVOO.
- Add the white wine and reduce by half. Add the tomato paste and cook out for a bit.
- Add tomatoes and seafood broth. Simmer for a while.
- Add juice and zest of lemon.
- Steam clams separately and set aside.
- Add shrimp and scallops at low heat and poach slowly.
- Once done, add the clams, butter, and fresh herbs.
- Add lump crab to top of bowls.
- Serve with bread for dipping or over linguini!
