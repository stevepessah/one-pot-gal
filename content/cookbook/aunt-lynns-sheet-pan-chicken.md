In the fall of 2017 Aunt Lynn received a Food & Wine magazine that would change her life and entertaining strategy forever. She made this sheet pan chicken for EVERYONE over the next few months, even making it several times in the same week. It is really, really yummy so who can blame her?

![Aunt Lynn’s Sheet Pan Chicken](images/aunt-lynns-sheet-pan-chicken-1.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 1/2 pound sourdough bread, cut or torn into 2-inch pieces
- 1/2 pound slab bacon, cut into 1-by- 1/2-inch pieces
- 1 large baking potato—scrubbed, halved crosswise and cut into 3/4-inch wedges
- 1 large red onion, cut into 1-inch wedges
- 2 tablespoons cold unsalted butter, diced
- 4 oregano sprigs
- 1/2 teaspoon crushed red pepper
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- Kosher salt
- Black pepper
- 6 whole chicken legs

## Instructions

- Preheat oven to 400°.
- On a large rimmed baking sheet, toss the bread, bacon, potato, onion, butter, oregano and crushed red pepper with the olive oil and season generously with salt and black pepper.
- Spread in an even layer.
- Season the chicken with salt and black pepper and arrange on the bread mixture.
- Roast the chicken and bread mixture for about 45 minutes, until the bread is crisp and an instant-read thermometer inserted in the chicken registers 160°.
