I can remember so many great after school days stopping by the Carre’s (unannounced of course) to find Aunt Lynn curled up with a bowl of Cheez-Its and a good book!

![Aunt Lynn’s Cheez-Its in a Bowl with a Good Book](images/aunt-lynns-cheez-its-in-a-bowl-with-a-good-book-1.png)

## Ingredients

- Cheez-Its (the regular kind)
- Small cute bowl

## Instructions

- Fill a small bowl with cheez-its, pick up a good book and cozy up.
- Wait for friends and family to stop by unannounced!
