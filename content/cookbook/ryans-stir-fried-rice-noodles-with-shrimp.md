## Ingredients

- 1 package (6 oz) rice sticks/noodles
- ½ lb medium shrimp; raw, peeled
- 1 tbsp vodka
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 4 shallots; chopped
- 1 tsp ginger; peeled, grated
- ½ lb napa or green cabbage; shredded
- 2 scallions; chopped
- 1 tbsp soy or tamarind sauce
- 2 c chicken stock
- ½ c fresh basil and/or cilantro
- salt and pepper

## Instructions

- Soak rice noodles in cold water 15-20 minutes (or according to package directions), until softened. Drain well, cut in half, and set aside.
- Dry the shrimp well, and marinate in the vodka.
- Heat oil over high heat in a wok or heavy skillet. Add the shallots and ginger, and cook over high heat until shallots are lightly browned. Add the shrimp and cook until pink.
- Add the cabbage, noodles, scallions, soy, and chicken stock. Cook 5 minutes until liquid is completely absorbed. Add herbs. Season with salt and pepper.
