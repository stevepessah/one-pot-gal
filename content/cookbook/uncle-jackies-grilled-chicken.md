### Best attempted

- In the Outer Banks
- Post- tennis match, beach day, or anything that will leave you famished and cranky
- When you have an entire day at your disposal

## Ingredients

- Chicken
- Coals
- Lighter fluid*

*exact recipe works better if you forget lighter fluid

## Instructions

- Light coals.
- Wait 7 hours.
- Order pizza.

***A note from Uncle Jackie: You’ll probably notice that none of my recipes are in this cookbook.***

*Let me explain. I was as talented a chef as anyone in the family. But, a harrowing experience cooking lunch for the entire family on a vacation in Duck, N.C. shattered my nerves so bad that I can hardly enter a kitchen—unless of course a buffet line is forming.*
