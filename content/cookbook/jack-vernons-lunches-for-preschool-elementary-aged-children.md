## Ingredients

- Leftover quarter pound cheeseburgers
- Stroehmann’s Wheat bread
- Apples or bananas

## Instructions

- Ideally, pack your 4-year-old a brown paper bagged lunch containing: ○ A leftover cheeseburger from last night between two slices of wheat bread. ○ An apple. ○ Allow your child’s aunt or uncle to swap this lunch for a can of Chef Boyardee or peanut butter and jelly.
- Voila! You’ve made several people happy.
