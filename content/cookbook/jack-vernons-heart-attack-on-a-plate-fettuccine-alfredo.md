Best served: In a kitchen next to a closed laundry room door and a trash can filled with empty soda cans. Yields 4 servings. For authenticity, try to get into an argument about academics, the Latin origin of a prefix, or the Eagles last loss.

![Jack Vernon’s “Heart Attack on a Plate” Fettuccine Alfredo](images/jack-vernons-heart-attack-on-a-plate-fettuccine-alfredo-1.jpg)

### Pairings

- Show musico
- A bottle of Budweiser

## Ingredients

- 5 1/2 oz. Butter, unsalted
- ⅛ cup Flour
- 1 cup Heavy cream
- 1/2 cup Parmesan, grated
- 1/2 cup Romano, grated
- 2 cloves garlic, mashef
- 8 oz. Grande Ricotta
- Kosher salt
- Coarse ground black pepper
- Parsley
- One lb Boxed fettuccine noodles

## Instructions

- Melt butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Sauté garlic.
- Add flour and cook for 2 to 3 minutes.
- Add heavy cream; stir constantly until butter-flour mix is incorporated and smooth.
- Add Parmesan, Romano, and Ricotta; stir until melted.
- Bring sauce to a simmer and cook for 7 to 10 minutes stirring constantly.
- Add salt and pepper to taste.
- Cool before serving, allowing sauce to thicken.
- For tradition, serve over cheap boxed noodles.
