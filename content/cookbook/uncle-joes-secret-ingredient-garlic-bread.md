It’s no secret that Joe Carre makes the best garlic bread. What is a secret is that he uses…(wait for it)...MAYONNAISE! But before you go judging this indulgent approach: Try it. You’ll never turn back!

![Uncle Joe’s Secret Ingredient Garlic Bread](images/uncle-joes-secret-ingredient-garlic-bread-1.jpg)

![Uncle Joe’s Secret Ingredient Garlic Bread](images/uncle-joes-secret-ingredient-garlic-bread-2.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 1 large baguette
- Mayo (MUST be Hellmann’s)
- Butter, maybe half a cup?
- Garlic, 4-6 cloves (eyeball it), minced or crushed into paste (garlic powder works too)
- Salt

## Instructions

- Preheat oven to 350.
- In a small saucepan, melt the butter, add garlic and heat through until fragrant. Remove from heat.
- Slice baguette lengthwise and spread a very thin layer of mayonnaise to both sides.
- Repeat with garlic butter mixture. Add salt. (Optional: add parsley and/or Parmesan cheese.)
- Place the two baguettes on foil, mayo/butter side up, and bake in the oven until warmed through, but still soft. At the end, broil on high for 30 seconds to get them browned and toasty!
- Serve alongside a delicious soup or pasta!
