These Bloody Marys are typically found at a “make-your-own” bar during Thanksgiving. Why are they being served at dinner? Because our parents were not part of the millennial “brunch” generation and completely disregarded the fact that this is a morning beverage.

## Ingredients

- V8 tomato juice
- Celery Salt
- Worcestershire Sauce
- Tabasco
- Celery
- Pepper
- Vodka
