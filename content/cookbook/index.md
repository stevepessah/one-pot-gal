## Decision ledger

1. The PDF’s built-in text came out with bullets detached from their lines.
   Guessed: read the file with PyMuPDF, which keeps each ingredient on its own line.
   Would have asked: Is a different extractor required?

2. The plan didn’t say where a photo sits inside the recipe.
   Guessed: after the backstory and before the ingredients, and also listed in the front matter.
   Would have asked: Should photos open the post, sit beside the story, or go at the end?

3. Cover, contributor, and part-divider photos aren’t tied to a recipe.
   Guessed: leave them out of `images/` and list the pages here.
   Would have asked: Should the cover and divider photos be saved too?

4. A few recipes use a one-word label such as Dirt or Sand between two methods.
   Guessed: keep those labels as small headings inside the same recipe.
   Would have asked: Should Dirt and Sand be separate posts?

5. Glossary photos sit beside the definitions and have no captions.
   Guessed: keep each definition intact and gather those photos at the end of the glossary.
   Would have asked: Should each glossary photo stay next to a specific saying?

6. The book doesn’t number the files.
   Guessed: add the PDF page numbers in front matter so each file can be checked against the book.
   Would have asked: Do you want page numbers stored with the recipe?
