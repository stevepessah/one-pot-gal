There’s never a reason to buy jarred sauce when homemade is so easy.

![Easy Marinara Sauce](images/easy-marinara-sauce-1.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 1 28 oz. can of tomato sauce
- 1 28 oz. can of crushed tomatoes
- Olive oil
- Chopped garlic, to taste
- 1 small vidalia onion, diced
- Red wine
- Basil
- Parmesan cheese, to taste
- Bay leaf
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions

- Heat a heavy bottomed pot on medium and add a generous helping of olive oil.
- Add the diced onion and saute until translucent.
- Add the garlic, saute until fragrant.
- Salt and pepper.
- Add a splash of red wine and allow to boil away.
- Add both cans of tomatoes.
- Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer.
- Add salt, pepper and bay leaf.
- Allow to simmer for at least 30 minutes.
- Right before taking off stove to serve, remove bay leaf, add parmesan cheese and basil.
