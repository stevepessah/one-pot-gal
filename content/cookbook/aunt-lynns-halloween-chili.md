It’s not a Carre family Halloween without a big pot of my mom’s chili. Green Acres was in its prime in the 80s/90s and the Carre household became the central hub for pre and post trick-or-treating. Cousins from both sides (Carre and Hoban) would come out for the festivities - and the house would be hustle-bustle with family, friends, costumes - and chili!

*Note from Lauren: You can also throw this into a bag of fritos with fixin’s for some walking tacos on 4th of July!*

![Aunt Lynn’s Halloween Chili](images/aunt-lynns-halloween-chili-1.jpg)

## Ingredients

- Wick Fowler's Texas Style 2 Alarm Chili Kit
- 1 onion
- 1 red bell pepper
- 1 green bell pepper
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- Package of ground beef
- 1 can black beans
- 1 can kidney beans
- 1 can white beans
- *White rice, cooked according to instructions
- For toppings: Sour cream, shredded cheese, cilantro, chives, and whatever else tickles your fancy!
- *fun fact: mom eats her chili over rice - something she picked up while living in Bangkok, Thailand in her 20s.

## Instructions

- Brown the meat and then drain the fat.
- Add the chili spice mix to the meat.
- In a separate pot, sauteé the onions and bell peppers.
- Add in the meat, 3 cans of beans (including the liquid from the beans), and the can of tomatoes (with juices).
- Simmer low for 1-2 hours. Serve over white rice, with toppings of your choice!
