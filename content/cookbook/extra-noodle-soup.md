## Ingredients

- 1 pouch Lipton’s extra noodle soup
- ½ cup elbow noodles (or small pasta of choice)
- Premium saltine crackers

## Instructions

- In a medium pot, bring 4 cups of water to a boil, add elbow noodles and simmer at a low boil for several minutes.
- Add the Lipton pouch and simmer at low until all the noodles are cooked through.
- Serve, and I can’t stress this enough, with Premium crackers.
