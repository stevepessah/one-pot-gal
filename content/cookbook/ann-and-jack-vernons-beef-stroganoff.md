Best made after a long day of work when you can’t bear to cook and have 5 mouths to feed.

*Note from Kathleen: I remember this being Jilly’s favorite growing up. I’d hear all about it! I believe it was requested by all 3 Vernon kids for various birthday dinners. It reminds me of the Vernon kitchen in Westwood Manor, the smell of the wood stove, and Uncle Jack. The Vernon’s always had a formal sit-down at their kitchen table, which was always really nice. They were fancy like that (they were also fancy when they answered the phone as “Vernon Residence,” and I think the first time I used a cloth napkin was at the Vernon’s!).*

## Ingredients

- 1 (8 ounce) package egg noodles
- 1 pound of the cheapest red meat you can find
- (10.75 ounce) can condensed cream of mushroom soup
- 1 tablespoon garlic powder
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Worcestershire sauce to taste

## Instructions

- Preheat oven to 350°.
- Prepare the egg noodles according to package directions and set aside.
- In a separate large skillet over medium heat, saute the beef over medium heat for 5 to 10 minutes, or until browned.
- Drain some fat and add the soup and garlic powder. Simmer for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
- Remove from heat and combine the meat mixture with the egg noodles in a CLASSIC TAN CORNINGWARE casserole dish.
- Bake at 350 for 10 mins.
- Add the sour cream, stirring well, and season with salt and pepper to taste.
- Ryan’s addition - add poppy seeds to the egg noodles! (That’s what I remember from my sleepovers!)
