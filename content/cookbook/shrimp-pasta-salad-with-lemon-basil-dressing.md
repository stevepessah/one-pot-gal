A Laura Pingatore recipe that quickly became a Hoban family staple. It has graced its presence at the infamous 4th of July parade in Havre de Grace. It has made an appearance at many a graduation, bridal showers, baby showers, and Easter Sunday. Creamy and zesty it is - with a pop of peas and all the noodles and shrimp your little heart desires!

## Ingredients

- 1 lb medium shell pasta
- 1 lb medium shrimp; cooked
- 1 c frozen peas; defrosted
- 1 c roasted red peppers (jarred in oil); chopped

### For Lemon-Basil Dressing

- 1 c Hellman’s mayonnaise (do not use light or fat free)
- ½ c fresh squeezed lemon juice
- 1 c fresh basil leaves; chopped
- 3-5 cloves garlic; minced
- Salt

## Instructions

- Mix dressing ingredients together in a large bowl, and refrigerate for flavors to meld.
- Cook pasta according to package directions for al dente. Drain, and rinse with cold water to stop the cooking process. Drain well.
- Add pasta, shrimp, peas, and roasted red peppers to the bowl with dressing. Mix well to coat.
- Refrigerate 2-3 hours, preferably overnight.
- If pasta seems dry the next day, add a little more mayo and lemon juice until creamy.
