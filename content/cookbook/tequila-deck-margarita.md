### Recipe with Uncle Chris present

## Ingredients

- Warm, bottom-shelf tequila
- Salt
- 1 shot glass for entire party to share
- A lime slice, roughly cut by a Swiss Army Knife

## Instructions

- Take the goddamn shot or get off the tequila deck.
- Watch out for bees!

### Recipe without Uncle Chris present

## Ingredients

- 2 oz chilled Tequila, preferably Espolon
- 1 oz freshly squeezed Lime juice
- 1 oz Cointreau

## Instructions

- Layer all ingredients into a shaker tin. Shake vigorously.
- Serve over ice, garnished with a lime and a salted rim, if you fancy.
