Need a break from Delaware/your life? Escape to Aunt Kate’s in the lovely Chesapeake bayside town of Havre De Grace, Maryland! There, you’ll be greeted with a bowl of chips and salsa and time to vent. If you’re of age, you’ll also get a glass of wine! Best enjoyed by the fire pit.

![Aunt Kate’s Chips and Salsa](images/aunt-kates-chips-and-salsa-1.jpg)

## Ingredients

- Restaurant-Style Tostitos
- Tostitos Salsa, mild

## Instructions

- Pour Tostitos chips into festive cactus bowl.
- Serve with salsa and a nurturing ear to listen to your loved ones vent!
