I always thought this was a hard recipe to make growing up, but gave it a try recently and it’s easy peasy! Best served on a chilly day with your favorite people.

![Aunt Wink’s New England Clam Chowder](images/aunt-winks-new-england-clam-chowder-1.png)

![Aunt Wink’s New England Clam Chowder](images/aunt-winks-new-england-clam-chowder-2.jpg)

## Ingredients

- ¼ lb bacon
- 1 medium yellow onion
- 4-5 medium potatoes
- 2 cups milk
- 3-4 small cans clams (save juice)
- 2 cups clam juice/water from cans
- Celery salt
- 2 T Flour

## Instructions

- In a large pot, cook chopped up bacon on medium heat until browned.
- Add onion and cook until soft (maybe 5 minutes).
- Add flour and stir.
- Add clam juice (whatever more you need to add to make it 2 cups can be water).
- Add peeled/ chopped potatoes and bring to a boil.
- Lower heat and allow to simmer until potatoes are soft.
- Add clams, celery salt and milk while soup is on low heat (Don’t let it boil once the milk has been added!).
- Add a lump of salted butter at the end.
