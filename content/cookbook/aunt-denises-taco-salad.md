4th of July just isn’t the same if mom doesn’t make her taco salad. Gourmet it’s not, but it has Doritos and is SO good! It also couldn’t be easier.

![Aunt Denise’s Taco Salad](images/aunt-denises-taco-salad-1.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs ground beef
- Your favorite taco seasonings
- 3 heads romaine lettuce
- 6 tomatoes, squeezed of their juices
- Shredded taco cheese
- 1 bag spicy doritos
- 1 bottle Catalina dressing

## Instructions

- Brown ground beef.
- Add taco seasonings and set aside.
- In a bowl combine chopped romaine, chopped tomatoes and cheese.
- Crunch up the doritos in their bags until they are small pieces.
- Before serving, add warm temperature ground beef, doritos and the Catalina dressing to the salad.
