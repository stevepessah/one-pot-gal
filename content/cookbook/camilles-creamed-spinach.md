Kathleen and Steve hosted their first ever Thanksgiving in the fall of 2019, and aside from bursting with gratitude and love for their incredible family, they were equally thankful to have tasted what is surely a new family favorite: Camille’s creamed spinach. In fact, it was so good that Joe Carre himself barged into the living room mid-meal to sing its praises!

![Camille’s Creamed Spinach](images/camilles-creamed-spinach-2.jpg)

## Ingredients

- 2 boxes frozen spinach (Camille uses Trader Joe’s)
- 1 8 ounce package cream cheese
- 1 cup Parmesan cheese
- 1 stick melted butter

## Instructions

- Grease oven safe casserole dish.
- Mix spinach, cream cheese, melted butter, and all but 2 tablespoons of Parmesan cheese.
- Pour into casserole dish and top with remaining cheese.
- Bake at 350 for 30 minutes or until bubbly.
